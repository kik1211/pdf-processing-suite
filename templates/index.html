Existing online PDF tools force you to jump between multiple websites, restrict batch operations, demand paid subscriptions for basic utilities, or risk your privacy by uploading confidential documents to remote servers.

"I HATE PDF" was built to bring all essential PDF utilities together under a single, fast, local application where your files stay completely on your computer.

“I was tired of using five different websites just to perform five different PDF operations.”
