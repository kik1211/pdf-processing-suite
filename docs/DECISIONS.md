# Architecture Decision Records (ADR)

Documentation of key architectural and design decisions made throughout the project lifetime.

---

## ADR Template

### Title
ADR-000: Short Title of the Decision

### Status
[ Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded ]

### Context
Description of the issue, requirement, or constraint driving the decision.

### Decision
The specific choice or architecture pattern adopted.

### Consequences
- Positive impacts and benefits.
- Trade-offs, drawbacks, or operational considerations.

### Alternatives Considered
- Option 1 and reason for rejection.
- Option 2 and reason for rejection.
